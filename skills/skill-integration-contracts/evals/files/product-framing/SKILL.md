# Product Framing

Starts from a feature idea or human-provided ticket. It can produce a provisional product brief containing goals, scope, requirements and open questions, including when no prior research is available.

Product intent decisions belong to the authorized product owner. The skill may receive approved edits from a human, but it does not publish or approve the product brief and does not automatically invoke another skill.

It may optionally share its output as context when a user chooses to supply it to another skill.
