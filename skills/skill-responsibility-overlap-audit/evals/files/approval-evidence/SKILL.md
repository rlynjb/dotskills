# Approval Evidence
Inspect a ticket, draft specification, or stakeholder decision. This review is read-only and does not approve requirements.

## Evidence labels
CONFIRMED REQUIREMENT: from a ticket, approved specification, or stakeholder decision.

## Boundaries
A ticket may contain unapproved or draft requirements. Preserve the source's stated requirement separately from its verified owner-approval status.
